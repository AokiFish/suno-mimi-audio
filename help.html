Mimi Audio

Play, capture, and save. Easily save complete audio from your Suno playback.

First-Time Setup

  1. After installing the extension, reload Suno and allow the extension to run on Suno if prompted.
  2. Open the extension popup, turn on Keep Capturing, then play or create a song.
  3. Click Mimi Audio in your browser toolbar and wait until the audio is ready.
  4. Edit the filename if needed, then click Save Audio. Your browser will handle the download location.

Important Notes

  • Captures belong to the Suno tab where the audio was played. Keep that tab open until you save your tracks.
  • Reloading the page may clear existing captures.
  • Turning capture off only stops new captures. Existing tracks remain available, and ongoing captures can finish. The toggle applies to all Suno tabs and is remembered automatically.
  • Up to 8 tracks can be saved at once, with a maximum size of 64 MiB per track.
  • Click X to remove an individual track, or Clear All to remove all captured copies.

Troubleshooting

A track isn't showing up?

Switch to another song, then play the desired song again. Tracks loaded before capture was enabled may be cached. If necessary, enable capture, reload Suno, and play the song again.

The extension can't connect?

Make sure you're on suno.com and that the extension has permission to run on the site. After installing or updating the extension, reload the Suno tab.

The captured audio is incomplete?

Incomplete captures cannot be repaired. Play the song again to create a new capture.

Future Compatibility

Changes to Suno's player, media hosts, or Workers may require an extension update.
