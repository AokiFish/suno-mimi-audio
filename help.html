MIMI AUDIO

Play. Capture. Keep.

A small companion for saving complete audio from your Suno playback.

Your first capture

  1. After installing, reload your Suno tab once. Allow the extension to run on Suno if your browser asks.
  2. Keep Capture is on enabled in the extension popup, then start a song.
  3. Open Mimi Audio from the browser toolbar. Wait for Ready to save.
  4. Edit the filename if you like and click Save audio. Your browser controls the save location.

A few things to know

Captures belong to the Suno tab where you played the songs. Keep that tab open until you have saved them. A reload clears its captures.

The pause switch stops capturing new playback. Existing captures stay available and any capture already in progress can finish. Your on/off preference applies to all Suno tabs and is remembered.

Up to four tracks, each at most 64 MiB, can be held at once. Remove a track with the × button, or use Clear all to release the captured copies.

If a track does not appear

Switch to another song, then play the desired song again. A track loaded before capture was enabled may be cached. If necessary, reload Suno while capture is on and then play the song.

If the popup cannot connect, check that you are on suno.com and that the extension has permission to run there. Reload the tab after installing or updating the extension.

An incomplete response cannot be repaired into a complete track. Retry playback for a fresh capture. Changes to Suno's player, media host, or use of Workers may require an extension update.

What stays on your computer

Audio is held in the page's memory until you save it. Playback keys stay in that page and are never exported. Only the on/off preference is stored by the extension.

There are no uploads, analytics, login-token reads, or additional license or download requests. Filenames and capture status are shown in the popup. The extension does not call Suno's download menu.

Mimi Audio is an independent local tool, not an official Suno extension.
